# TEAM.md

## Track 3: VR for Experiments

This team is building configurable VR environments for controlled experiments in COGS 160 Track 3. The goal is to create reusable VR scenes that can be modified through parameters and AI-assisted workflows, then connected to experimental tasks, behavioral measurement, and Knowledge_ATLAS.

## Pipeline

Assets → VR Environment → Parametric Control → Experiment → Data → Knowledge_ATLAS

## Roles

### 1. VR Environment Architect
**People:** 2–3

**Role Definition:**  
Builds and designs VR environments in Unreal Engine. Responsible for scene layout, scale, lighting, navigation flow, interaction points, and overall usability of the environment. This role also includes interaction and user-experience design inside the VR scene.

**Members:** TBD

### 2. Asset Pipeline Engineer
**People:** 1–2

**Role Definition:**  
Handles the asset pipeline from source models into usable VR assets. Responsible for taking assets from sources such as Sketchfab, Meshy, or Tripo3D, cleaning them in Blender, fixing scale and materials, and importing them correctly into Unreal Engine.

**Members:** TBD

### 3. AI & Parametric Systems Designer
**People:** 2–3

**Role Definition:**  
Defines which environmental variables can be adjusted and how AI-driven modification should work. Responsible for parameter logic such as ceiling height, lighting, wall shape, window size, and similar configurable scene properties. Also responsible for designing prompts or instruction templates used with the AI room-modification workflow.

**Members:** TBD

### 4. Experimental & Data Systems Designer
**People:** 2–3

**Role Definition:**  
Designs the experiment logic inside VR and defines how user behavior will be measured. Responsible for hypotheses, independent and dependent variables, user tasks, data logging structure, behavioral measurements, and how measurements are captured during interaction with the environment.

**Members:** TBD

### 5. Knowledge & Integration Engineer
**People:** 1–2

**Role Definition:**  
Connects the entire Track 3 workflow to Knowledge_ATLAS and ensures coherence across all stages of the system. Responsible for mapping environmental features to psychological mechanisms and behavioral outcomes, aligning scenes with experiment goals, and integrating outputs into a structured knowledge framework.

This role structure is designed to match the Track 3 workflow of VR environment creation, parametric modification, experimentation, measurement, and integration into Knowledge_ATLAS. Roles may later be refined as the project develops and team assignments become clearer.
